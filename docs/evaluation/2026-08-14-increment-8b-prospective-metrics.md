# Increment 8B prospective metrics and ablation

Issue #464 implements deterministic measurement of an already frozen fixture
qualification Run. It does not start evaluation, fetch a source, use a
provider, or authorise live shadow, canary, publication, or production work.

## Evidence boundary

`newsroom.increment8.metrics` retains count, denominator, sampling method,
uncertainty, frozen threshold, and deterministic parts-per-million result for
every rate. Coverage is expressly bounded to the reviewed prospective
universe and is never described as absolute real-world recall.

Eight material geography, language, urgency, and failure-heavy slices are
required. Each needs at least 12 completed Cases and must independently meet
the frozen slice threshold. Missing exposure is `NOT_EVALUATED`; aggregate
success cannot hide either a missing or failed slice.

Zero-tolerance findings, latency and amplification limits, source contribution,
rights, cost, search Purpose, and provider-version evidence remain separately
attributable. Source decisions guard against removing a quiet Anchor or
promoting a high-volume Comparator without event-level value.

Ablations report lost or earlier detection, resilience, noise, cost, and
affected slices. They are comparative only and cannot rescue a failed target.
Every report records exact code, environment, sample and label manifests and
retains deviations as digests safe for the public repository.
