## Owner decision

The owner explicitly authorises the SDLC hard budgets to be doubled for all tiers. Newsroom risk tiers R0 through R4 share the same gate and lane hard ceilings, so this amendment applies uniformly to every tier without changing risk classification, required lanes or evidence escalation.

The accepted hard ceilings are amended as follows:

| Boundary | `sdlc-v2.2` | `sdlc-v2.3` |
|---|---:|---:|
| Global gate command | 55 s | 110 s |
| Global interactive lane | 55 s | 110 s |
| Finalisation | 5 s | 10 s |
| Route command | 5 s | 10 s |
| Source-integrity command | 15 s | 30 s |
| Core deterministic command | 55 s | 110 s |
| Authenticated Neo4j service command | 55 s | 110 s |
| Merge-exact command and lane | 55 s | 110 s |
| Science shard | 55 s | 110 s |
| Evidence-finalize command and lane | 5 s | 10 s |

The contract and watchdog retain a strict upper bound below 120 seconds. Any later increase still requires another explicit owner amendment and a new contract version.

## Preserved controls

This amendment changes hard enforcement ceilings only. It does not:

- change the p50 or p95 performance targets;
- remove, select away, skip or reclassify any required test;
- weaken exact-tree, exact-lock, exact-toolchain or evidence identity checks;
- permit a fake or no-op service to satisfy authenticated Neo4j evidence;
- change SQLite, governed-object, projection or publication authority;
- authorise Graphiti, a model, embeddings, live sources, credentials, spending, publication, canary, shadow product execution or production activation; or
- allow a rerun to overwrite the first required result.

A command that exceeds its amended ceiling remains `BUDGET_EXCEEDED`. Unknown paths and contract-control changes continue to escalate to R3.

## Implementation authority

The owner authorises one reviewed change to update the machine contract, contract-version binding, watchdog validation, signed workflow operational caps, tests and specification. The exact changed head must still pass every applicable repository, Authority, Projection, authenticated Neo4j and signed SDLC gate before merge.

## Rollback

Rollback is one reviewed revert of the `sdlc-v2.3` amendment commit. That restores `sdlc-v2.2` and the previous hard ceilings. Evidence produced under one contract version cannot satisfy another because the contract version and contract-controlled source tree are bound into routing and evidence identities.
