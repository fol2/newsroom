## Authority

The additive readiness v3 record preserves the frozen numerical Evaluation
Plan, Operational Profile, migration history and non-effect boundary while
authorising the final fixture qualification attempt. It does not authorise a
live provider, runtime credential, runtime external egress or spend,
publication, shadow, canary, permanent locality or production activation. The
manual exact-main workflow has one read-only GitHub control-plane query to
retain the merged PR's exact-head substantive-review authority.

## Exact artifacts

The decision job first validates the exact SHA/tree, PASS core/service
transports, 13 frozen closeout cases and retained merged-PR review evidence.
The review record binds the merge SHA, reviewed source head, Codex review ID
and zero unresolved P1/material-P2/other threads; bypassed or unreviewed merges
fail closed. It then executes the reviewed 120-Case closed-world fixture and
retains the complete canonical `QualificationPacket` and the packet-bound
`OperationalAdmissionDecision`. The Increment 8 receipt reconstructs both,
binds their digests, the retained substantive-review digest and the exact
original-registration Handoff anchor, and records only the non-activating
fixture verdict and separate-plan eligibility. Qualification uses a production
module and remains executable in the locked no-development environment.

The signed-closeout job independently checks both canonical subjects, their
packet/decision relationship, owner separation, non-activation fields and
receipt identities before attesting the raw review authority, packet,
admission decision, Increment 8 receipt and the existing exact-main
decision/closeout subjects.
