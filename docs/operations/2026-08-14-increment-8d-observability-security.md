**Canonical language:** UK English

## Boundary

Increment 8D creates canonical fixture evidence for health, coverage, observability, incidents and security admission. It performs no request and authorises no live credential, network egress, external spend, shadow, canary, publication or production activation.

## Health and coverage

Health retains authority, schedule, transport, parser, freshness, semantic-integrity, downstream and budget dimensions. Healthy unchanged requires a complete successful observation under the exact contract. Freshness is measured from the last complete success; the last source change remains a separate time.

Coverage is evaluated per accepted obligation, role and path dependency. An Active obligation whose Anchor has no credible healthy current-authority path becomes `COVERAGE_BLOCKED` and invokes scoped containment. A Comparator or search path never repairs a failed Anchor.

## Access and input security

The fixture access contract fixes HTTPS, approved hosts, redirect, TLS, timeout, body-size, content-type, decompression and parser-safety limits. It contains zero credential references and zero egress destinations. Conditional `304`, empty `2xx`, not-found, gone, rate-limit, redirect, failure and shape-drift outcomes retain distinct meanings. Shape drift produces a quarantine outcome rather than a publisher change.

Source or model content is evidence only. It cannot alter policy, tools, authority, egress or budgets. Manual retry, requeue, quarantine release, contingency and override receipts require authenticated actor, authorisation and evidence digests and are always audited.

## Observability and incidents

Observability records bind exact source, component, Profile, provider and policy versions. They cover schedule, complete-success age, outcome, parser, retry, queue, budget, coverage, storage and reconciliation and correlate due trigger through Handoff using digests only. Alert priority is derived from integrity, coverage and urgency consequences and binds an owner, escalation path and versioned runbook.

Material incidents retain an immutable open, contained, recovered and closed chain. Closure retains root cause and follow-up; integrity errors and material near misses also require an Evaluation regression Case digest.

## Rollout posture

Security admission is exact-version scoped. Rights, terms, pricing, credential scope, rollback, scoped disable and mandatory GraphRAG readiness each fail closed. Canary support is represented for later planning, but canary and production activation remain false.
